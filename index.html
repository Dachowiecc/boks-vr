Ring Tsubasa

Headset: press ENTER VR below, then punch a target to pick a mode.
Combos: red pads are your left hand (L), blue pads your right (R). Numbers are boxing calls: 1 jab, 2 cross, 3 lead hook, 4 rear hook, 5 lead uppercut, 6 rear uppercut.
Orange bar: roll under it. Yellow wall: slip your head the way the arrow points.
Don't Get Hit: your rival throws punches, slow at first, then faster. Slip, duck or block with your gloves. Five clean hits and you're out.
Desktop: left / right mouse button or F / J for left / right hand (hold to block in Don't Get Hit). C to duck, A / D to slip.
